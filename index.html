Who it's for

Three sides. All three get paid.

Every settlement pays the AI company that served the call, the protocol, and the app that brought the buyer. Nobody signs up anywhere.

For AI companies · list once

Customers you never onboarded.

  • No signup, no card, no key. Install the middleware and your first settled call lists you.
  • Keep what you earn, in USDC, to your wallet. Your endpoint, your brand, your ERC-8004 identity.
  • Apps are paid to bring you buyers. Buyers stake on you, ranking you first.
For buyers · one balance

Every tool, one balance, one MCP server.

  • Fund from any wallet or on-ramp. Nothing changes in your tools.
  • No account anywhere. Pay per call, not per month, at prices the supplier's saved costs allow.
  • Session keys and spend caps, set locally. Earn $FORAGE on every call you pay for.
For the apps that bring buyers · get paid

A builder code on every call you route.

  • A fee the user approves once and can revoke, paid at every settlement.
  • Claimed from the treasury, no invoices. A public scoreboard of who brings paid volume.
  • Earn $FORAGE on the usage you route, on top of the builder fee.
$FORAGE · proposed

Usage earns it. Holding discounts it. Staking directs it.

A fixed number of $FORAGE is minted each week and split by recognized usage among buyers, AI companies and the apps that bring them. No day-one unlock; the team is on the same clock. The mint halves as usage doubles, and the unearned part is burned.

1

Weekly mint

A fixed number each week. No day-one unlock. Halves as cumulative usage doubles; what is not earned is burned.

2

Split by usage

Every settled call earns $FORAGE: buyer, supplier and app, each. Recognized usage only; wash proofs first.

3

Buyers hold

More held, bigger discount on every AI company's credits. Stake mints a daily call credit and a lower fee tier at settlement.

4

Buyers stake on sellers

Stake $FORAGE in any supplier's pool. Rewards follow that supplier's usage; stake directs part of the mint there. Longer lock, more rewards.

5

More calls

Cheaper for holders, better-ranked sellers. Staked suppliers rank first and pay less; apps are paid to route buyers.

6

Fees buy $FORAGE

A share of fees buys $FORAGE back; bought tokens fund the credit pool. Buybacks above the mint shrink the mint. Early unstake and proven wash are slashed.

Built on open standards

The call runs at the edge. Base holds the money.

Open services anyone can run do discovery, settlement and accounting. Our code is the catalog, the attribution and $FORAGE.

x402

Payment and settlement

Signed vouchers per call; a facilitator verifies for free and settles in batches, one transaction per batch. Anyone can run one; we run one.

ERC-8004

Identity and reputation

Every tool is a registered agent. Reputation with receipt hash, written at listing. Validation badges; TEE later.

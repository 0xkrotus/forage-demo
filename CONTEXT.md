# Forage glossary

**Buyer.** The person or agent paying for tool calls. In the demo, Mia's research agent. Avoid: user, customer.

**App.** The product the buyer uses, which routes the call and earns a cut for bringing the buyer. In the demo, Scout. Avoid: builder, router, referrer.

**AI company.** The business selling a tool through Forage. In the demo, SearchCo. Avoid: seller, provider. (Antseed calls this the seller.)

**Vault.** Where the buyer's USDC sits locked until it settles. One balance pays for any tool.

**Running total.** The amount the buyer has signed as owed to one AI company. It only goes up. Each new signature replaces the last; the AI company keeps only the latest.

**Settlement.** The AI company cashing in the latest running total. One settlement pays three payees: the AI company, Forage and the app.

**Fee.** Forage's share of a settlement, 4%. The app's share is the app cut, 5% in the demo.
